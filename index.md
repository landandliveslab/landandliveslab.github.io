Our research investigates how we can better manage ‘working’ landscapes to restore and maintain biodiversity and ecosystem function. 
​
On a planet facing significant ecological challenges—including deforestation, biodiversity loss, and climate change—we need a suite of conservation and restoration approaches to reduce human impacts and rebuild responsible ways of living. 

While protected areas are considered the gold standard in conservation, they will never cover sufficient area to mitigate intensive land use elsewhere, and often adversely impact the local and indigenous peoples who are already caring for these lands. We must therefore improve the conservation value of all landscapes, and especially of highly modified landscapes such as those devoted primarily to agricultural production. 

**Because the ecological success of conservation efforts depends on people’s support and engagement with these efforts, our research is interdisciplinary, collaborative, and seeks conservation approaches that are just, equitable, and meet the needs of both nature and people**.
