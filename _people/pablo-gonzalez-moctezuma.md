As a multidisciplinary researcher, my interests range from the abstract knowledge and methods of complexity science, systems thinking, and mathematical models to the particularities of functional ecology, agroecology, and rural development. During my PhD I am exploring who the actors doing restoration in Latin America are and what their respective drivers are. My fieldwork takes place in southern coastal Mexico.

Using agroforestry plots deployed as part of the national policy Sembrando Vida, I want to explore smallholder farmers' perceptions of restoration and systematize the way they make decisions on the design and management of their plots. I want to understand if functional diversity is related to the success of restoration initiatives, and examine the factors that allow a small farmer to become an agent of restoration.

In my free time you can find me fishing, especially if salmon are running, walking around the forest, or learning how to sail.
