The global community has made huge commitments to forest landscape restoration — initiatives like the Bonn Challenge target 350 million hectares of degraded and deforested land worldwide. Our lab works in Malawi, Ecuador, Mexico, India, and here at home in British Columbia to understand how restoration and land-use change unfold on the ground, and how we can balance ecological outcomes with social ones.

We take a spatial approach to these problems, seeking to understand how landscape structure affects ecosystem function. We collect and combine data from many sources, including historical records, ecological fieldwork, socio-economic surveys, satellite imagery, and maps. We work with academics across disciplinary divides, with local communities, and with NGOs, in the tropics and at home.
